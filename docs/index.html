Everything you copied, still there.

Clipboard and screenshot history for the macOS menu bar. Press ⇧⌘V anywhere, find it, paste it. No account, no network, no telemetry.

Download for Mac
$ brew install ricardus/tap/clipstack

Free and open source (MIT). macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon.

    What it keeps

    A clipboard history should know what to forget.

    Recorded

    • Text, links and coloursTagged with the app they came from.
    • Images and filesDrag them back out into any app.
    • New screenshotsPicked up from your screenshot folder the moment they appear.

    Ignored on purpose

    • Anything a password manager marks as concealedThe pasteboard flag 1Password and friends set so history apps skip them.
    • Everything copied from excluded apps1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass, Dashlane, Enpass, Keychain Access, Apple Passwords. Add your own.
    • The networkNo sync, no analytics, no update pings. History stays in Application Support.
